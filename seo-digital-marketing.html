Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About SEO & Digital Marketing

What is SEO?

SEO means Search Engine Optimization. It involves improving website content and technical structure to help search engines understand pages and potentially show them for relevant searches.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Results vary according to competition, website condition, content and the work involved. SEO usually requires ongoing effort, and specific rankings or timelines cannot be guaranteed.

Can you help an existing website?

Yes. The first step is to review the existing website and identify suitable improvements based on its current structure, content and objectives.

Does digital marketing include paid advertising?

Digital marketing can include paid advertising, but this page focuses mainly on SEO, website optimization, strategy and performance measurement. Advertising campaigns can be discussed separately.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No. Search engine rankings depend on many factors outside any one provider's control. We focus on sound practices, useful improvements and measurable progress.
