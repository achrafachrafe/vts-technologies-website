Frequently Asked Questions

What is custom software development?

It is the process of building software for specific business needs rather than relying only on a ready-made solution.

Can you build a system for my business?

Yes, the first step is to discuss your goals, required features, users and budget to determine a suitable solution.

Can you connect the software to a database?

Yes. Database integration can be included when appropriate for the project, helping you store and manage information.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the scope, complexity and features. A realistic estimate can be provided after discussing the requirements.
