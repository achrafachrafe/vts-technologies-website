FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a website need maintenance?

Maintenance helps identify bugs, review updates, improve performance and keep the website aligned with changing requirements.

Can you fix a website created by another developer?

In many cases, yes. The first step is to review the website, available source code and access permissions to determine what can be fixed.

Can you help with a website that is not working?

Yes. Share the error message and relevant details so the issue can be assessed and the possible next steps identified.

Do you offer regular maintenance?

Recurring maintenance can be discussed according to your needs, the website's technical setup and the tasks agreed in advance.

How much does maintenance cost?

Pricing depends on the type of issue, the amount of work required and whether you need a one-time fix or recurring support. Contact us to discuss your project.
